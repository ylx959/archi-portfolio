About YLX Studio

Design is not about finding the right answer.
It is about creating a better one.

As artificial intelligence continues to transform the way we create, originality becomes more valuable than ever. Technology can generate endless variations, but true design is defined by vision, intention, and the ability to imagine new possibilities.

This portfolio showcases a selection of architectural works and visual explorations developed throughout my university studies. More than a collection of projects, it represents a journey of curiosity, experimentation, and creative growth—revealing how ideas evolve into meaningful spaces, experiences, and stories.

YLX Studio is an independent design practice working across spatial design, image direction, and visual storytelling.

The studio works as a crossing point between disciplines, where spaces, images, and atmospheres come together through clarity, mood, and a distinct aesthetic sensibility.

Yang Linxuan

Background: NTUST B.Arch | Incoming USYD MCS

Combining spatial thinking, user experience, and emerging technologies to create meaningful and engaging human experiences.
